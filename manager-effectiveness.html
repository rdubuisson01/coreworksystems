Manager Effectiveness Program

Solve the manager-performance problem you have now.

A focused engagement for organizations where managers are already in role, expectations are reasonably clear, and execution is still inconsistent.

Corework focuses on one defined performance challenge, helps managers strengthen the practices they need to address it, and gives leadership a clearer view of what may be helping or getting in the way of stronger performance.

The problem

When manager performance becomes a business problem.

Accountability is weak. Managers struggle to delegate and make decisions at the right level. Difficult performance conversations happen too late. Teams struggle with competing priorities.

The question underneath the workWhat do these managers need to do differently, and what is getting in the way when they try?

The Manager Effectiveness Program may be a fit when:

  • Execution varies significantly from manager to manager
  • Managers avoid or delay difficult performance conversations
  • Managers struggle to delegate and make decisions at the right level
  • Leadership is unsure whether the issue is manager capability, the surrounding environment, or both
How it works

Built around one real challenge.

Every engagement begins with a focused look at the presenting problem through a sponsor conversation, a short manager pulse, and targeted review of the information needed to understand the challenge. From there, the work moves through a simple cycle.

  1. BeforeManager Context Scan
  2. 01Diagnose & Focus
  3. 02Practice & Adjust
  4. 03Solve the Hard Cases
  5. 04Sustain & Transfer
  6. AfterInsight Readout

Managers apply what they are learning to real situations while the program is underway. The content changes with the challenge, whether the issue is accountability and feedback, delegation and decision authority, prioritization, team execution, or another defined performance problem.

The goal is stronger manager practice and a clearer understanding of the conditions affecting performance.

What you get

A Manager Effectiveness engagement includes:

A focused Manager Context Scan
Four facilitated cohort sessions built around the identified challenge
Practical tools and real-work application between sessions
A Manager Effectiveness Insight Readout with recommendations for managers and leadership

The result is a clearer view of what managers can strengthen directly, what may be getting in their way, and what leadership should address next.
