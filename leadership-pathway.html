Corework Leadership Pathway

Build the leadership capability required at each level of responsibility.

Leadership changes as responsibility grows. Managing people directly requires different capabilities than leading through other managers. Senior leaders face another shift as their decisions, influence, and accountability extend across functions and the enterprise.

The Corework Leadership Pathway gives organizations a deliberate way to prepare people for those transitions through cohort-based development tied to the work leaders are actually responsible for.

When leadership development needs a clearer path

Organizations often invest in leadership development without creating a clear progression from one level of responsibility to the next.

New managers may enter the role without a strong foundation. Experienced managers may struggle when their success begins to depend on other leaders. Senior leaders may need to expand beyond functional expertise into broader enterprise judgment.

The question underneath the Pathway is:

What do leaders need to be able to do differently as their responsibility grows?

The Leadership Pathway may be a fit when:

  • → You are promoting strong individual contributors into management
  • → Leadership expectations vary significantly across levels or teams
  • → Development programs feel disconnected from the work leaders actually do
  • → You want a clearer pipeline for preparing people for greater responsibility

One pathway. Three levels of leadership.

Level I | Managing People
New and emerging managers

Build the foundational capability to create clarity, delegate effectively, develop people, address performance, and lead a strong team.

Level II | Leading Through Others
Experienced managers and leaders of managers

Strengthen the ability to translate priorities through other leaders, develop management capability, and lead consistently across teams.

Level III | Enterprise Leadership
Senior directors and emerging executives

Build the judgment, cross-functional influence, talent leadership, and enterprise perspective required as leadership scope expands.

Organizations can begin with the level where the need is greatest or build toward a broader multi-level pathway.

How it works

Each level is built around the work leaders are responsible for at that stage.

Participants learn a small set of practical approaches, apply them to real work between sessions, return with actual experience, and adjust based on what happened.

Learn → Apply → Reflect → Adjust

For private cohorts, Corework works with the sponsor to understand the leadership population, current expectations, and operating context so the experience reflects the organization's reality.

The goal is leadership development that changes how people actually lead in practice.

What you get

Depending on the level and scope, a Leadership Pathway engagement includes:

  • → Facilitated cohort-based development
  • → Practical tools tied to the work leaders are responsible for
  • → Structured application to real leadership situations
  • → Sponsor insight into recurring leadership challenges and development priorities

The result is a stronger foundation for how leaders operate now and how the organization prepares people for greater responsibility over time.
