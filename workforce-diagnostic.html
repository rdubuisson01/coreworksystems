Workforce System Diagnostic

Find where the strategy is breaking between intent and execution.

Organizations can invest heavily in a workforce strategy, platform, framework, or rollout and still see little change in how decisions get made, how managers operate, or whether results improve.

The Workforce System Diagnostic helps senior leaders identify where the system is breaking down, why the change is not taking hold, and what should be addressed first.

The problem

When the strategy makes sense, but the system isn't delivering.

The initiative may be well designed. Leaders may support and champion it. People may have invested significant time and resources. And yet operating practice has not changed enough.

The question underneath the workWhere is the system actually breaking, and what should we fix first?

The Diagnostic may be a fit when:

  • Adoption or implementation is lagging despite significant investment
  • New expectations have not changed day-to-day decisions or manager behavior
  • Leaders disagree about whether the problem is ownership, capability, execution, or something else
  • The organization knows results are off track but cannot confidently identify where the system is breaking down

This applies across workforce and talent transformations, including AI-enabled workforce change, skills-first strategies, mobility, workforce planning, and talent-system redesign.

How it works

From evidence to diagnosis to action.

Corework reviews the available evidence behind the strategy, including agreed documents, stakeholder perspectives, and relevant system data.

We examine six parts of the infrastructure underneath execution:

1
OwnershipWho is accountable for results, and who can resolve issues when progress stalls?
2
Capability ArchitectureWhat do people need to be able to do, and what evidence shows they can do it?
3
Decision ArchitectureHow do the strategy and capability expectations shape actual hiring, development, promotion, and investment decisions?
4
Manager Operating ConditionsDo managers have the authority, information, time, and support to act on what is expected?
5
Operating IntegrationIs the change built into everyday workflows, routines, and responsibilities?
6
Measurement & AdaptationCan the organization see what is changing, learn from the results, and adjust?

The work then moves from evidence to diagnosis to action.

The goal is to distinguish symptoms from causes, so leaders know where intervention is most likely to matter.

What you get

A standard Diagnostic provides leaders with:

A clear view of where the system is breaking down
Findings across the six dimensions, grounded in the evidence available
Clear priorities for where to focus first
A practical 30/60/90-day roadmap for what to address next

The result is greater confidence about what is actually preventing the strategy from becoming operating practice, and where to act first.
