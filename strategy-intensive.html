Workforce & Talent Strategy Intensive

Turn an important workforce or talent priority into a strategy you can act on.

Major workforce priorities often arrive before the organization has resolved what they actually require. Leaders may agree on the ambition while holding different views about the priorities, tradeoffs, ownership, or changes needed to make it real.

Corework works with senior leadership teams to turn consequential workforce and talent priorities into a focused strategy with clearer decisions, ownership, and a practical path forward.

Senior leadership team· 1–2 day intensive· Pre-work and alignment scan· Clear path forward

When the priority is clear, but the path forward isn't

A new business direction, AI initiative, workforce shift, talent challenge, or leadership mandate can create pressure to act before the underlying choices have been worked through.

Teams may be dealing with too many priorities, unclear ownership, competing assumptions, or broad goals that have not yet been translated into operating decisions.

The question underneath the work is:

What should we actually do, who owns it, and what has to change to make it work?

This engagement may be a fit when:

  • → Leaders agree something needs to change but are not aligned on what comes first
  • → A major initiative needs a workforce or talent strategy underneath it
  • → Competing priorities are making execution difficult
  • → Ownership, dependencies, or implementation requirements remain unclear

How it works

Before the leadership team convenes, Corework does focused pre-work to understand the priority, current assumptions, ownership, dependencies, and likely barriers to execution.

The intensive then moves the team from broad intent to concrete choices.

Clarify the Priority → Surface the Choices → Align on Ownership → Build the Path Forward

The goal is a strategy the leadership team can actually act on after the session.

What you get

Depending on the challenge and scope, Corework helps the leadership team leave with:

  • → A clearer set of workforce or talent priorities
  • → Explicit decisions and tradeoffs
  • → Clearer ownership and responsibilities
  • → A practical set of implementation priorities and next steps

The result is greater clarity about what the organization will do, who will own it, and what has to happen next.
