About Corework

The person behind the work

A career spent helping people grow. And organizations get better at it.

Richard began his career in the classroom. Over more than 25 years, his work has grown to include building learning systems, developing leaders, and helping employers rethink how they hire and advance talent.

He brings that experience to Corework: a practical understanding of how people learn, what managers face, and what organizations need to change for good ideas to take hold.

Why Corework exists

Good intentions need
a way to work.

A leadership program can give managers new skills. They still need the time, authority, and support to use them. A commitment to internal mobility can open possibilities. People still need a fair, consistent way to show they are ready.

Corework grew out of seeing these gaps across education, workforce development, and employer advisory work. We help organizations develop their people and improve the decisions, expectations, and practices that shape what those people can do.

The question behind our work: what needs to change so people can put their capabilities to work?
